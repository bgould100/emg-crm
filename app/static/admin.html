Account provisioning checklist

Before you send login details:

  • Confirm the contractor profile is correct.
  • Use a unique, strong temporary password.
  • Send credentials privately, not by public message.
  • Confirm the contractor can sign in successfully.
This screen does not store login passwords in browser storage. Once submitted, the password field is cleared.
